## Highlights

10 PRs merged and 47 commits — the heaviest merge week outside W30, and almost
entirely bug-fixing.

**Blockfrost hardening.** Two fixes landed: HTTP errors are now retried instead
of crashing the node (#2729, closing #2477, where the node died immediately on a
Blockfrost 5xx), and datum decoding was fixed (#2754). A follow-up issue was
filed to replace a blind sleep with condition-based polling (#2753).

**Persistence and rotation.** Pending deposits now survive a restart after
rotation (#2758), and a VACUUM race between rotation and API history streaming
was closed (#2755). The checkpoint-replay bug that dropped `pendingDeposits` and
`chainPointTime` (#2642) was closed alongside them.

**Test infrastructure.** The `hydra-node` test suite is now parallelised
(#2761), and nightly CI was caught silently running zero tests (#2756) — the
kind of failure that looks green for months. The `PersistentQueue` deadlock fix
opened back in W26 also merged here (#2742), as did the non-canonical inline
datum round-trip fix (#2746).

Two issues opened that matter later: a head closed with sub-min-ADA dust outputs
can never be fanned out (#2759, closed in W33), and fanout failing with H9
`NoOutputDatumError` on a closed head (#2751, opened and closed the same week).
HD wallet keys (`PaymentExtendedKey`) are now accepted in `hydra-node` (#2583).

None of the 18 items carried a workstream milestone — this predates the
milestone reorganisation — so all of it reads as Reactive.
