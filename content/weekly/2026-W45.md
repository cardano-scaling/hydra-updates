## Highlights

Five PRs merged this week, increasing Head capacity, fixing snapshot and
persistence stalls, and improving the documentation. Four further PRs
opened. There was no new Hydra release during the reporting period.

**Ecosystem Support (M4).** The [snapshot capacity increase](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2932)
merged Monday, raising the maximum from 4,095 to 32,767 outputs in response
to a builder's request. It uses a larger trusted setup from the Ethereum
KZG ceremony. The limit includes outputs owed by pending commits and
decommits alongside the Head's UTxO set. The setup switch is marked as a
breaking change for the next release.

**Operational Excellence (M3).** The [capacity stall fix](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2927)
merged Friday, closing #2924. Previously, a transaction could push the Head
over its accumulator limit and trigger a snapshot request that every party
rejected, leaving further snapshots blocked. Snapshot construction now
reserves capacity for pending commits and decommits, defers deposits that
do not fit, and rejects expanding decommits that depend on unconfirmed
merges. `TxValid` still reports local ledger acceptance; confirmation comes
with inclusion in a snapshot.

The [SQLite and startup improvements](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2929)
also landed. Event writes are split into batches of at most 1,000, fixing
a deadlock when a batch exceeded the bounded write queue. Startup skips
unnecessary event replay and streams required history in bounded batches,
reducing the amount held in memory. Test filtering now fails when it selects
no tests, preventing a misleading successful result.

**Maintenance and DevX (M5).** The [transaction guide](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2935)
now explains why Plutus transactions inside a Head need collateral even
when fees are zero, resolving the reported `NoCollateralInputs` issue.
Collateral must come from the Head's own UTxO set; a suitable spending
input can also serve as collateral. The [contributor guide](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2938)
was refreshed with current binary caches, a faster edit-and-test workflow,
and the checks needed before submitting changes.

**Next.** All four PRs opened this week remained open on 8 November.
The [transaction payload cleanup](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2931)
would release pruned transaction bodies while allowing pending snapshots
to recover missing payloads through retransmission. A separate
[snapshot stall report](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/issues/2936)
reproduces a transaction expiring between local acceptance and snapshot
processing; that issue remained open at the week's end.

The [user-defined policies proposal](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2933)
would let builders constrain deposits, minting, allowed assets and UTxO
count, with every participant required to use the same policy. This follows
the request for application-level validation in #2913.

A draft [HeadLogic refactor](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2939)
explores composing state machines with Crem and separating protocol
decisions from their execution through an interpreter. The accompanying
[review guide](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/blob/8dd93f7102cea4076e647a5aaf44d68eff4461e8/docs/head-logic-review.md)
connects the rules to generated diagrams, reproducible traces and
compatibility tests, making snapshot, deposit and recovery behaviour
easier to inspect. The draft retains existing network and persistence
formats; performance costs and recovery policies remain part of the
review. Work also opened on the [documentation publishing workflow](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2937).
