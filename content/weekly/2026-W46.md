## Highlights

Seven PRs opened this week as the HeadLogic refactor moved into a staged
review. No PRs merged and no new Hydra release was published during the
reporting period.

**Operational Excellence (M3).** Last week's refactor sketch was split into
smaller changes: [pinning Crem](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2942),
[separating runtime and data boundaries](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2943),
introducing a [typed program interpreter](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2944),
and [moving protocol rules into typed workflows](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2945).
This makes the rules for snapshots, deposits and decommits easier to review
separately from execution and persistence. The proposed state owners make
responsibility for pending transactions and snapshots explicit, with checks
that live execution, event replay and checkpoint recovery agree.

The refactor also includes proposed safety corrections: preserving validated
transaction bodies when duplicate IDs arrive, retaining decommit reservations
when the local ledger is rebuilt, and checking snapshot confirmations against
the candidate and participating parties. The original
[refactor PR](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2939) became the
final step in the stack, addressing acknowledgement and settlement ordering
that could otherwise leave unspent inputs unavailable. These changes remained
under review at the week's end.

**Maintenance and DevX (M5).** An
[independent regression suite](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2946)
was opened alongside the refactor. It compares bounded scenarios with the
executable Agda reference models and checks capacity limits, conflicting
reservations, snapshot verification and recovery from retained events and
checkpoints. This provides explicit protocol expectations against which to
check the new implementation.

A separate [test reliability PR](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2949)
addresses failures exposed by CI: a queue test could cancel a write before it
reached disk and then wait indefinitely on reload; WebSocket examples could
spend their transport deadline generating large fixtures; and a logging test
required too many sequential pipe round trips within a fixed deadline.
Focused tests and node-test lint checks passed locally. Full Nix validation
remained dependent on CI because required build dependencies were unavailable
locally.

**Next.** Review the refactor stack, complete CI and benchmark comparisons,
and investigate the offline snapshot report. The six new stack PRs remained
open at the end of the reporting period; the separate
[refactor-only PR](https://github.com/cardano-scaling/hydra/pull/2941) was
closed without merging on 12 November.
