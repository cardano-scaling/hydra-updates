## Highlights

5 PRs merged, 7 others opened and 47 commits across two repos, with no
releases. Two issues were opened and closed within the week: #2876, fixed
by the node bump below, and #2886, the acknowledgement-signature problem
#2884 addressed, closed along with it. One merge is last week's test
migration; the other four are a cardano-node bump, a fix to how the node
decides its network is backed up, a new delegated-head demo, and a
changelog tidy. The seven other PRs were all closed unmerged before the
week ended.

**Reactive.** The test migration described last week (#2875) merged Monday.

cardano-node moved from 11.0.1 to 11.1.2 (#2877, merged Wednesday, closes
#2876), on the suspicion that the old version was behind a nightly failure.
The demo docker-compose image, which had been left on 10.6.2, was bumped
too. The same PR scaled the 900-second per-test timeout by the same 3x
factor as the Blockfrost waits, which had been cutting a nightly Blockfrost
test off early, and fixed a flaky "reports a stalled broadcast" test, which
now scrapes metrics with retries.

The node's network outbox reported `BacklogFull` at a fixed 1000 pending
messages, so under constant load a node refused `NewTx` while its network
was still draining normally. In the end-to-end benchmarks each refused
transaction then invalidated every later one in its client's chain ("All
inputs are spent"), which is why the sustained-load and plateau scenarios
had been failing on master. The outbox now also judges the backlog by how
long it would take to drain at the recent completion rate, and reports
`BacklogFull` only once that exceeds `noProgressFor`; the depth cap stays
as a memory backstop, raised from 1000 to 10000 (#2889, merged Friday).

A new delegated-head demo (#2888, merged Friday) runs a head whose
operators commit no funds of their own: two independent addresses
increment funds into it, transact, and leave by decrement. One of them
builds its deposit transaction in JS and submits it straight to L1, to show
the hydra-node API isn't required for depositing. It runs with
`nix run .#delegated-demo`. The changelog was also tidied (#2887, Thursday).

Seven stacked PRs (#2878 to #2884), four on rollback handling and three on
snapshot refusals, acknowledgement signatures and a deviating-party model,
were opened Wednesday and closed Thursday and Friday without merging.
