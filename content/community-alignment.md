The first deliverable of the initiative is to align the ecosystem around a single
developer-experience (DevX) strategy and to map the tooling that already exists,
so every other deliverable builds on shared foundations rather than reinventing
them.

## Why alignment first

Cardano's DevX is fragmented: capable tooling, documentation, and libraries exist,
but they are built in isolation, don't interoperate cleanly, and offer no
"blessed path" for a newcomer. Before building anything new we need a shared
picture of what exists, who maintains it, and where the gaps are — otherwise we
risk duplicating effort or contributing work that never lands.

## Principles

- **Build with, not around.** Coordinate with the teams who own each tool and
  contribute upstream; everything that can be upstreamed, will be.
- **Neutral coordination.** Act as a neutral party that connects existing efforts
  rather than competing with them.
- **One blessed path, many stacks.** Give newcomers a clear default while keeping
  the ecosystem's diversity of tooling.
- **LLM-grounded.** Treat documentation and examples as inputs to coding agents,
  not just human readers.
- **Measure honestly.** Tie the strategy to outcomes we can verify (see the
  program's impact measures).

## How this connects to the deliverables

- **cardano-init** leverages the mapped tooling so a new project is set up in
  minutes.
- **Contracts Library** and **Developer HUB** build on the same inventory to give
  developers battle-tested starting points and a single entry point.
- **Community Collaboration** funds improvements to the highest-impact tools the
  map surfaces.
- **Reactive** work addresses gaps the alignment process reveals.
